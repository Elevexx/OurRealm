# OurRealm — Community Standards

**Effective date:** February 18, 2026
**Version:** 2026-02-18

> Be authentic, be kind, and don't post content that endangers, exploits, deceives, or harasses others.

## Prohibited content & conduct
- **Spam & scams** — deceptive, fraudulent, or repetitive content; phishing; financial scams.
- **Harassment & bullying** — targeting, dogpiling, or repeated unwanted contact.
- **Hate speech & threats** — attacks based on identity (race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, etc.); threats of violence.
- **Sexual exploitation** — non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual coercion, trafficking.
- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** — strictly prohibited; reported to authorities; account terminated.
- **Self-harm promotion** — content that encourages or glorifies self-harm or suicide.
- **Impersonation** — pretending to be another person or entity in a deceptive way.
- **Privacy violations** — doxxing, sharing private information without consent, non-consensual recording.
- **Copyright infringement** — see [DMCA_POLICY.md](./DMCA_POLICY.md).
- **Illegal activity** — content that promotes or facilitates illegal acts.

## Enforcement
Depending on severity, history, and context, violations may result in:
1. Content removal or hiding
2. Temporary feature restrictions
3. Account suspension
4. Account termination

Users see one of two notices on actioned content:
- _"This content is under review."_
- _"This content was removed for violating Community Standards."_

## How we review
OurRealm combines in-app user reports with lightweight automated scanning (keyword, regex, URL, repeat-offender signals) into a single moderation queue. Admin reviewers act on items in the queue. We follow a **reasonable-efforts** model — not all content is reviewed proactively, and no automated system is perfect.

For full process detail: [SAFETY_POLICY.md](./SAFETY_POLICY.md).

## Report something
Use the in-app **Report** button on any post, comment, profile, message, or media. For urgent safety issues: safety@ourrealm.social.
