# OurRealm — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** February 18, 2026
**Version:** 2026-02-18

> **Beta notice:** OurRealm is a beta platform. Features may change, be modified, or be removed without notice.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account information** — email, password hash, signup timestamp, OTP verification records.
- **Username & profile information** — name, username, bio, avatar, banner, customizations, interests, mode, public links.
- **User-generated content** — posts, images, videos, sounds, comments, reactions, hashtags, saved items, shares, Groups, Realms.
- **Messages** — friend-only DMs, including support tickets in conversations with `@support`.
- **Media uploads** — files plus metadata.
- **Support tickets** — subject, category, status, conversation contents, optional screenshots.
- **Device information** — OS, application version, locale.
- **Browser information** — user agent, viewport size, browser type/version.
- **IP addresses** — for security, abuse prevention, and approximate location.
- **Approximate location** — derived from IP at city/region level. No precise GPS without explicit prompt.
- **Usage analytics** — pages and features used.
- **Crash logs** — exception and error reports.
- **Security logs** — sign-in attempts, failed logins, brute-force lockouts, session tokens.
- **Moderation logs** — admin actions (Approve, Hide, Restore, Delete, Ban User) and audit data.
- **Report data** — reason, optional detail, optional screenshot evidence.
- **Cookie and session information** — see [Cookie & Tracking Notice](#cookies).

## 2. How We Use Information
Operate, secure, and improve OurRealm; create and maintain your account; deliver friend, messaging, and presence features; personalize the feed; respond to support tickets; enforce policies; detect, investigate, and prevent abuse and security incidents; comply with law.

## 3. Sharing
We do **not** sell personal information. We share data with:
- **Service providers** — vendors required to operate the platform under contract.
- **Other users** — only as you direct (public posts, friends-only posts, custom audiences).
- **Law enforcement and authorities** — when required by valid legal process or to protect users, rights, property, or safety.

## 4. Vendor Inventory
Categories of service providers (specific vendors may evolve; this section is updated on material changes):
- **Hosting & infrastructure** — cloud compute, ingress, and edge networking.
- **Database services** — managed MongoDB.
- **Authentication** — first-party JWT and OTP today.
- **Storage** — uploaded media storage (today ephemeral pod storage; future durable cloud object storage).
- **Realtime / messaging** — Supabase Realtime.
- **Analytics** — first-party in-app analytics; third-party analytics may be added with prior notice.
- **Email delivery** — transactional email (OTP, password reset, security notices).
- **Customer support** — internal admin ticketing; future third-party CRM may be added with prior notice.
- **Payment processing** — **not currently active.**

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
Not directed to children under 13. Age confirmation required at signup. Underage accounts will be removed when discovered. Parents/guardians: privacy@ourrealm.social.

## 6. Your Rights
Access, edit, and delete your information from Account Settings. Email privacy@ourrealm.social to:
- **GDPR (EU/UK):** exercise access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California):** know, request deletion, correct, opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell).

## 7. Data Retention
- **User accounts** — while active; on deletion the visible profile is removed and the account row is anonymized.
- **Messages** — while both participants exist and the conversation has not been deleted.
- **Support tickets** — while the linked account exists; closed tickets may be retained for audit.
- **Moderation audit logs** — up to **90 days** unless needed for active investigation.
- **Security logs** — short security windows.
- **Internal webhook event logs** — up to **30 days**.

We do **not** claim immediate or irreversible deletion. Deleted content may be retained for limited periods for legal compliance, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and platform security.

## 8. Security
HTTPS / WSS in transit; password hashing; brute-force lockouts; role-based admin permissions; audit logging on admin actions. No system is perfectly secure. The Service does **not** claim end-to-end encryption.

## 9. Legal Disclosures
We may disclose information in response to valid subpoenas, court orders, search warrants, or preservation requests, or when we believe in good faith disclosure is necessary to protect users, rights, property, or public safety. Internal workflows are not published.

## 10. International Transfers
Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you live.

## 11. Changes
Material changes are highlighted in-app or by email.
